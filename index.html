Need Assistance?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your stay, amenities, bookings, and location at The Foothill Farmstay.

What are the check-in and check-out times?
▾
Standard check-in time is from 12:00 PM onwards and check-out time is until 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out options are available subject to room availability and prior notice.
Where is The Foothill Farmstay located and how do I reach there?
▾
We are nestled in the serene elevated landscapes of Daringbadi, Odisha ("The Kashmir of Odisha"). We offer assistance with private transfers from Berhampur / Bhubaneswar railway stations or airports upon request.
What types of cottages and accommodations are available?
▾
We offer four handcrafted luxury cottage categories: Deluxe Cottages, Superior Cottages, Family Suites, and Premium Mountain View Cottages — each featuring private sit-outs, king beds, and modern luxury amenities.
Are meals included in the stay?
▾
Yes! We offer farm-to-table dining experiences featuring authentic local Odia cuisine, North & South Indian dishes, and customized dining plans upon request.
What activities and amenities are available for guests?
▾
Guests can enjoy our private swimming pool, campfire & barbecue sessions, pine forest and coffee plantation tours, tribal cultural events, conference facilities, and expansive lawn spaces.
How can I book or check room availability?
▾
You can book directly via WhatsApp (+91 8249659996), call our reservations team directly, or fill out the enquiry form in the contact section below.
