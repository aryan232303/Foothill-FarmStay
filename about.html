About The Farmstay

Welcome to
The Foothill Farm Stay

Nestled in the serene hills of Daringbadi, Odisha — affectionately known as the Kashmir of Odisha — The Foothill Farm Stay is more than just a hotel; it is a peaceful retreat deeply connected with nature, culture, and warm countryside hospitality.

Set against gentle foothills and surrounded by lush mountain greenery, our farm stay brings you closer to the natural beauty and indigenous heritage of this unique hill station. Inspired by local traditions, we have thoughtfully designed our property to offer a stay that feels authentic, tranquil, and deeply memorable.

From the refreshing aroma of local coffee blossoms and the rich heritage of organic turmeric cultivation to the surrounding pine forests, rolling hills, and cool mountain air — every element of Daringbadi has inspired our vision. Our aim is to let guests experience the destination, not simply visit it.

Whether you seek a quiet personal sanctuary, a romantic getaway, a memorable family holiday, or a venue for grand celebrations, The Foothill Farmstay delivers uncompromised comfort surrounded by nature.

Our Philosophy

"Stay close to nature. Connect with culture. Leave with memories."

We believe true hospitality is about making guests feel entirely at home while giving them something they cannot experience anywhere else.
